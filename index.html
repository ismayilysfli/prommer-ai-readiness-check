AI Project Readiness Check

Answer six quick questions to see whether your idea needs more preparation, a small pilot, or is ready to move into implementation.

1. Is there a clearly defined business problem?
2. Is there a measurable success metric?
3. Is the required data available?
4. Is there a person responsible for the outcome?
5. Can a human review or override the AI output?
6. Is there a realistic way to test the idea on a small scale first?
